Privacy, in plain words.

This policy applies to the WireBench iPhone app and this website. The developer is Peng Lin. Contact: 540933120@qq.com.

1. Data we collect through the app

WireBench does not require an account and does not send app analytics, identifiers, request content or usage history to a developer-operated service. The app contains no third-party analytics SDK. Data Studio transformations run on your device.

2. Connections you initiate

When you start a request or connection, the address, headers, payload and network information needed for that connection are sent directly to the server you specify. That server and network providers may receive your IP address and process or retain data under their own policies. DNS queries use your device's network configuration. Choose endpoints you trust and send only information you intend to disclose.

HTTP, WS, plain TCP and UDP do not provide transport encryption. HTTPS, WSS and TCP with TLS use system certificate validation. WireBench does not intercept other apps' traffic and does not run a VPN. Local network permission is used to connect to development devices and servers that you choose; you can change it in iOS Settings.

3. Local records and retention

Saved endpoint configurations and up to 100 recent activity summaries are stored in the app's local container. Request headers and bodies are not saved with configurations. Recognized URL credentials are redacted. Activity history does not retain response bodies or exchanged messages. Session logs are kept in memory with bounded limits and are discarded when their view is released or the app exits. You can clear a session, delete a saved configuration, clear history, or use Settings → Delete all local records.

Redaction is a heuristic and cannot recognize every secret or business field. Review URLs before saving and content before sharing. Local records may be included in device backups according to your Apple backup settings. Uninstalling the app removes its current local container; separate device backups are managed through Apple settings.

4. Sharing and support

Sharing is initiated by you through the iOS share sheet after a preview. The destination app or recipient then handles the shared text. Contacting support opens your email application. If you send an email, we receive the address and information you include and use them only to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send access tokens, passwords or private payloads. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request and any required recordkeeping. Email us to request deletion.

5. Website hosting

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web request information, including your IP address and browser request headers, to GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement. This site does not use analytics, tracking cookies or contact forms. The language switch runs in your browser without storing a preference.

6. Changes and questions

If data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. For questions or support data requests, contact 540933120@qq.com.

清楚说明如何处理数据。

本政策适用于 WireBench iPhone 应用与本网站。开发者：Peng Lin。联系方式：540933120@qq.com。

1. 应用中的数据处理

WireBench 不要求创建账号，不向开发者运营的服务发送应用分析、标识符、请求内容或使用历史。应用不集成第三方统计 SDK。数据工坊的转换在你的设备上完成。

2. 你主动发起的网络连接

当你发起请求或连接时，地址、请求头、载荷以及建立连接所需的网络信息会直接发送给你指定的服务器。该服务器及网络服务商可能接收你的 IP 地址，并按各自政策处理或保留数据。DNS 查询遵循设备的网络配置。请选择可信端点，仅发送你打算披露的信息。

HTTP、WS、普通 TCP 和 UDP 不提供传输加密。HTTPS、WSS 及启用 TLS 的 TCP 使用系统证书校验。WireBench 不截取其他 App 的流量，不运行 VPN。本地网络权限仅用于连接你选择的开发设备和服务器，可在 iOS 设置中调整。

3. 本地记录与保留期限

收藏端点与最多 100 条最近操作摘要保存在应用本地容器。保存配置时不保存请求头或请求体，并对识别出的 URL 凭证脱敏。历史记录不保存响应体和收发消息。会话报文只在有限额的内存中保留，在相应页面释放或应用退出后丢弃。你可以清空会话、删除收藏、清空历史，或进入“设置 → 删除全部本地记录”。

自动脱敏无法识别全部秘密或业务字段。保存前请检查 URL，分享前请检查内容。本地记录可能根据你的 Apple 备份设置包含在设备备份中。卸载应用会删除当前本地容器；独立的设备备份请在 Apple 设置中管理。

4. 分享与支持邮件

分享由你主动发起，在预览后通过 iOS 分享面板发送，之后由目标应用或接收者处理。联系支持会打开邮件应用。若你发送邮件，我们会收到邮箱地址和邮件中包含的信息，仅用于回复和排查问题。请勿发送访问令牌、密码或私密报文。支持邮件仅在处理问题及必要记录所需期间保留，你可以发送邮件申请删除。

5. 网站托管

本静态网站托管于 GitHub Pages。访问时，IP 地址、浏览器请求头等普通网页请求信息会传送给 GitHub。请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。本站不使用统计分析、追踪 Cookie 或联系表单。语言切换仅在浏览器运行，不保存偏好。

6. 政策更新与问题

数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页及生效日期。如有问题或希望处理支持邮件中的个人信息，请联系 540933120@qq.com。
